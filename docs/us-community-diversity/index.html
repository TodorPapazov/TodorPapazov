Do more diverse US counties also show the local conditions in this hypothesis?

Click a county to zoom in and read that community. The map colors one measure at a time. The panel places the county among counties of a similar size and shows how home values, the mix of who lives there, the foreign-born share, and unemployment have moved. These are published numbers side by side. They do not show that diversity caused an outcome, and they do not rank counties as better or worse places to live.

A community is a county in the 50 states or the District of Columbia with at least 50,000 residents in the 2019-2023 American Community Survey. Smaller counties stay on the map in gray. Who lives there is the effective number of racial and ethnic groups. Born outside the United States is the foreign-born percent. The two scores are never averaged. Comparisons use three size bands: 50-150k, 150-500k, and 500k or more. The widest gap between race and ethnicity group counts and the county total is 0.0000. High school graduation is a ninth-grade cohort's four-year completion, not a test score. County highway spending is that government's own direct highway spending. A blank means the source did not publish a figure.
