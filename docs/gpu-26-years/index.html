BRIEF // AFFORDABILITY STORY

What the numbers say

01 · INFLATION

Nominal lies a little

A $599 card in 2006 is not the same burden as $599 in 2024. CPI adjustment puts every launch on a 2025 footing so “expensive” means purchasing-power expensive.

02 · YEAR KPIs

Median real by launch year

Rank years by the median real MSRP of major cards that launched then. Red bar = most expensive year; green = most affordable. Change filters to stress-test mid-range eras.

03 · VALUE

$/GB and maker mix

Real price per GB of VRAM tracks long-run value. Manufacturer medians (NVIDIA vs AMD vs Intel) show pricing posture — not identical SKUs, but directional telemetry.
